About the system

Everything your organisation needs to test students on computers

OKV CBT turns one organisation computer into an exam server. Students sit exams on any computer or phone connected to the same network - no internet, no data cost, no risk of an exam failing because of a bad connection.

  • Objective, theory or both in one exam - the organisation decides.
  • Theory answers can be typed or written on paper (hard copy) and scored by teachers.
  • Students sign in with Matric or Admission Number plus Surname.
  • Automatic marking, optional instant scores, positions and report sheets.
  • Your logo, colours and student photos on every screen and printout.
