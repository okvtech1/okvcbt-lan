Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data OKV Technology Consults ("OKV", "we") collects through this website and the OKV CBT software, why, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Who is responsible for your data

For this website, forms and licensing, OKV Technology Consults is the data controller. For the data inside OKV CBT (students, staff, scores), your organisation is the data controller and OKV does not host, access or receive that data. It stays on the organisation computer.

2. Data we collect through this website

  • Free-trial form: name, organisation, email, phone, role, number of students, Windows version.
  • Installation and contact forms: name, organisation, email, phone, location, notes.
  • Purchase and activation forms: the above plus payment amount, date, reference and the payment receipt you upload, and your software Install ID.
  • Paystack payments: your name and email are sent to Paystack. We receive confirmation and a reference, never your card details.
  • Technical data: pages visited and cookie choices stored in your browser.

3. Why we use it

  • To give you the download and send installation instructions.
  • To verify payments and issue and email your activation code.
  • To provide support, installation and training.
  • To contact you about your request, and to keep records for accounting and legal duties.

Our lawful bases are your consent (forms), performance of a contract (licence, installation) and legitimate interest (support, fraud prevention).

4. Who we share it with

We use Google (Google Sheets, Drive and Gmail via Apps Script) to store submissions, receipts and send emails, and Paystack to process online payments. We do not sell personal data. We may disclose data if required by law.

5. Data inside OKV CBT

The software stores student and staff names, ID numbers, photos, answers, scores and messages in a database file on the organisation server. Organisations must have a lawful basis to process this data, keep it secure, make backups and respect the rights of students and parents. Administrators can edit and delete any record from the dashboard.

6. Retention

We keep form submissions and payment records for as long as needed for support and for accounting and legal requirements (generally up to 6 years for payment records), then delete or anonymise them.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent and to receive a copy. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Security

We restrict access to our Google account and records. Staff passwords in OKV CBT are stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure; keep your passwords and backups safe.

9. Children

This website is for adults. Student data in OKV CBT is entered and controlled by organisations, who must obtain any required parental consent.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Continued use means you accept the update.
