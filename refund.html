Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

We want you to be sure before you pay. That is why there is a free 12-day trial.

1. Try before you buy

Please use the free trial to confirm OKV CBT suits your organisation and computers before paying.

2. When you can get a refund

  • We cannot deliver your activation code within 24 hours of a complete submission and you ask for your money back.
  • You paid twice for the same installation.
  • The software cannot be made to work on your Windows computer after our support team has tried to fix it for you, and you request a refund within 7 days of receiving your code.

3. When refunds are not given

Once an activation code has been delivered and used, the licence fee is otherwise non-refundable. Installation, training and on-site services are non-refundable once the work has started.

4. How to ask

Email technologyokv@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 810 414 1138 with your name, organisation, payment reference and reason. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method or account within 7 to 14 working days. Paystack or bank charges may not be refundable.

5. Cancellation

You can cancel a purchase before your code is issued. Nothing recurs because there is no subscription.
